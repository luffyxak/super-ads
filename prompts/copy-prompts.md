# Copy Prompts — Hooks, Scripts, Captions

Use these to draft, then edit in your own voice. Never ship the first draft.

## Hook generator
"Write 10 hooks for [product] for [audience]. Each hook is 6 words or fewer, spoken in second 1–2, in [Hinglish / English]. No greetings, no 'Introducing'. One hook must be a subversion, one must be emotional, one must be a demo. Rank them by scroll-stopping power and explain the top 3 in one line each."

## Script writer
"Write a 15-second script for [concept]. Beats: 0–2 hook, 2–6 setup, 6–12 twist/demo, 12–15 payoff + CTA. About 35 words total. Dialogue the way [audience] talks. Captions for every line. CTA in the last 3 seconds only: [CTA]."

## 30-second script
"Write a 30-second script for [concept]. Beats: 0–2 hook, 2–8 setup, 8–20 twist/demo, 20–27 payoff, 27–30 CTA. About 70 words total. One joke per beat. No corporate words. Captions for every line."

## Hinglish adapter
"Rewrite this script in natural Hinglish for [Tier-2 city / metro]. Keep the joke, do not translate word for word. Use the words the audience actually uses. Mark any line that sounds forced."

## Caption writer
"Write captions for this script. Two lines max per caption, 32 characters per line, high contrast. Sound-off must still work. No wall of text."

## Social copy
"Write post copy for this ad: one post line, one meme line, one comment prompt. Each is one line. The meme line must be shareable as a screenshot."

## Moonshot-style rewrite
"Rewrite this script in Moonshot style: celebrity subversion not endorsement, deadpan delivery, medium is the joke, challenger energy. The star is the joke, not the pitch. Cut 20% of the words. Make the first line a hook, not a greeting."

## Diwali rewrite
"Rewrite this script for Diwali: family, ritual, gifting, light. The product is the guest, not the host. Hinglish, warm, Tier-2/3 clarity. Offer with a real end date: [offer]."

## CTA tester
"Write 5 CTAs for [product]. Each is one action, with where and by when. Rank them by clarity. Kill any CTA that is vague."

## Quality check prompt
"Read this script out loud, timed. Is the hook in second 1–2? Is there one punch? Is the CTA only in the last 3 seconds? List 3 cuts to make it 20% shorter."
