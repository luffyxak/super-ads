# Product Launch Prompts

## Launch strategy prompt
"Write a launch plan for [product] in one page: audience, one punch, proof, price, launch date, CTA. The punch is 5 words max. The proof is one fact with a source. The CTA is one action with where and by when."

## 10-second launch video
"9:16 vertical, 10 seconds, [product] is missing for 7 seconds, everyone looks for it, then it appears in the hero's hand, camera pushes in, 24fps, clean premium look. Negative: no text in frame, no warped label."

## 15-second launch reel
"Write a 15-second launch reel for [product]. Hook in second 1–2. The product appears once, clearly, in use, for 2–3 seconds. No hard sell before 80%. CTA in last 3 seconds: [CTA]. About 35 words."

## Teaser (3 days before)
"Write a 6-second teaser for [product]. No product shown. One line, one sound, one date. The audience must ask 'what is this?' — not 'what is the price?'"

## Reveal (launch day)
"Write a 15-second reveal for [product]. The product lands at second 10. Before that, tension only. After that, payoff + CTA. Captions for every line."

## UGC launch
"Write a UGC brief for 5 creators for [product]. Each creator gets one angle, not the same script. One angle is a demo, one is a reaction, one is a comparison, one is a day-in-life, one is a joke. No creator reads a brand script."

## Price reveal
"Write a price reveal for [product] at ₹[price]. The price appears once, big, clear, for 2 seconds, at the payoff. No 'up to', no asterisk without the real number in the same frame."

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Punch is 5 words max
- [ ] Product appears once, clearly, in use
- [ ] Price is true, dated, and in the brief
- [ ] Teaser, reveal and UGC are different, not the same ad cut three ways
- [ ] CTA is one action, with where and by when
- [ ] Consent and licences are in the delivery folder
