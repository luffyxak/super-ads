# Ad Creative Director

## When to use
- After strategy, before script.
- When the first idea is the obvious one. Kill it and find the second idea.
- When an ad feels like every other ad in the category.

## Workflow
1. Read the strategy page. Do not start with the product shot.
2. Write 3 concepts, each in 3 lines: hook (first 2 seconds), twist, payoff.
   Make them different in kind, not in wording. One emotional, one funny, one subversive.
3. For each concept, name the meme: what frame or line gets screenshotted?
   If there is no meme, the concept is not shareable. Fix it or kill it.
4. Pick 1. Kill 2, in writing, with one reason each. "Safe", "seen before", "needs a celebrity to work".
5. Check the Moonshot test: would Tanmay Bhatt's team laugh at this, or yawn?
   Celebrity subversion, not endorsement. Medium is the joke. Challenger energy.
6. Check the 50-year test: will this still make sense in 5 years, or is it only a trend?
   Trends are seasoning, not the meal.
7. Write the concept board: hook, 3 beats, payoff, CTA, look in one line, sound in one line.
8. Hand off to the copywriter with the board. Do not write the full script yourself.

## Output format
- Concept A / B / C: hook, twist, payoff, meme frame
- Picked: one concept + why
- Killed: two concepts + why
- Concept board: hook, beats 1-2-3, payoff, CTA, look, sound

## Quality bar
- The hook could not be swapped into a competitor's ad.
- The twist happens by second 5, not second 25.
- The payoff earns the CTA. No hard sell before 80%.
- The ad is rewatchable. A second watch reveals a joke you missed.

## Examples
- Fintech Moonshot-style: A serious actor angrily explains a simple thing.
  Twist: the anger is the joke. Payoff: the product is so simple even he calms down.
- Jewellery Diwali: A family argues about parking, walls, pets — a box of sweets ends it.
  Twist: the gift is the peace offering, not the product shot.
- Product launch: The product is missing for 20 seconds. Everyone looks for it.
  Twist: it was in the hero's hand the whole time.

## Anti-patterns
- A concept that needs a 30-second explanation.
- A concept that only works if the viewer already loves the brand.
- A celebrity reading a script. That is an endorsement, not a subversion.
- Three concepts that are the same idea with different words.
