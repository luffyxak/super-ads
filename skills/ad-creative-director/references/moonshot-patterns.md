# Moonshot patterns — reference for ad-creative-director

Patterns learned from Moonshot / Tanmay Bhatt and the challenger school.
Inspired by the patterns only — no ad scripts copied.

## Five patterns
1. Celebrity subversion, not endorsement: take a beloved, calm celebrity and make
   them do the exact opposite of their image. The surprise is the joke.
2. The medium is the joke: the format, the platform, or the buy itself carries
   the punch — a remote prank, a renamed brand, an apology that becomes the ad.
3. Internet-native writers, not TV writers: comedy and meme logic first, polish second.
4. Short, rewatchable, meme-able: 15-30 seconds, one punch, built to be shared.
5. Challenger energy: punch up at the category leader, the boring default, the
   expensive incumbent — never down at the viewer.

## The Moonshot test
Would this team laugh at the concept, or yawn? If the concept needs the celebrity
to work, it is an endorsement. If the concept works and the celebrity makes it
funnier, it is a subversion.

## Kill rules
- Kill a concept that needs a 30-second explanation.
- Kill a concept that only works if the viewer already loves the brand.
- Kill three concepts that are the same idea in different words.
