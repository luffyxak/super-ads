# Look sheet — reference for ad-visual-designer

## Feeling first
Name the feeling in one word: warm, cheeky, premium, chaotic. Everything else
serves that word.

## Palette
Three colours max: one hero colour, one warm or skin tone, one dark for text.
Diwali jewellery: deep maroon #7A1F2B, gold #D4A017, cream #FFF6E5.
Fintech challenger: black #111111, yellow #FFD400, white #FFFFFF.

## Type
One display face for the hook, one clean face for captions. Large, bold,
readable on a phone in sunlight. No thin fonts at small sizes.

## Framing (9:16 first)
Hero in the centre third. Text inside the 168-1330px band — nothing in the top
168px, nothing below 1330px except texture. Minimum 28px type, about five words
per claim line. Frame 0 is the thumbnail: a face, a result, or one bold line
already on screen. Never black, never a logo.

## Styleframes
Three frames — hook, twist, payoff — each must work as a thumbnail still.
If it does not work still, it will not work moving.
