# Ad Copywriter — Gen-Z

## When to use
- After the creative director picks a concept.
- When the script sounds like a press release.
- When captions are missing, or the ad does not work sound-off.

## Workflow
1. Write 5 hooks first. Each hook is 6 words or fewer, spoken in second 1–2.
   Test them out loud. If you would scroll past it, kill it.
2. Pick the best hook. Write the script in beats, not paragraphs:
   0–2s hook, 2–8s setup, 8–20s twist / demo, 20–27s payoff, 27–30s CTA.
   For 15s: 0–2, 2–6, 6–12, 12–15.
3. Write dialogue the way people talk. Hinglish where the audience speaks Hinglish.
   Short sentences. One joke per beat. No jargon.
4. Write captions for every line. Captions on, always. Sound-off must still work.
   Captions: 2 lines max, 32 characters per line, high contrast.
5. Write 3 caption / thumbnail lines for social: the post copy, not the ad copy.
   One line that can be a meme, one line that can be a comment, one line that can be a share.
6. Read the script out loud, timed. 15s is about 35 words. 30s is about 70 words.
   Cut 20%. Then cut again.
7. Hand off to the visual designer with script + captions + hook variants.

## Output format
- Hooks: 5 lines, 6 words or fewer each
- Script: timed beats table (time, visual, audio, caption)
- Captions: full caption file text
- Social copy: post line, meme line, comment prompt
- Word count and runtime: e.g. 68 words, 29 seconds

## Quality bar
- First line is a hook, not a greeting. No "Hello friends", no "Introducing".
- No word the audience would not say. No "leverage", "ecosystem", "seamless".
- The joke lands in the script, not only in the performance.
- The CTA is one action: "Shop now", "Download", "Visit the store", with where and by when.

## Examples
- Hook: "My dad just roasted my bank." (fintech)
- Hook: "This gold? My nani approved." (jewellery Diwali)
- Hook: "We hid the product for 20 seconds." (launch)
- Hinglish line: "Arre, ye to 2 tap mein ho gaya." — keep it natural, do not force it.

## Anti-patterns
- A hook that is a question with no tension: "Do you love gold?"
- A script that explains the product for 20 seconds.
- Captions that are a wall of text. Two lines, then cut.
- A CTA in the middle. CTA belongs in the last 3 seconds only.
