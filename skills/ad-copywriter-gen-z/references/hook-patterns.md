# Hook patterns — reference for ad-copywriter-gen-z

## Five hook shapes
1. The roast: someone trusted says the uncomfortable truth ("My dad just roasted my bank.").
2. The subversion: a calm person does the opposite of their image.
3. The missing product: everyone looks for it; it was in the hero's hand.
4. The family tension: a small argument only a gift can end.
5. The demo: the product does the impossible thing in two taps, on camera.

## Rules
- Five hooks first, six words or fewer each, spoken in seconds 1-2.
- 15 seconds is about 35 words; 30 seconds about 70 words. Cut 20%, then cut again.
- Captions: two lines max, 32 characters per line, high contrast, sound-off works.
- Hinglish where the audience speaks Hinglish. Never translate word for word.
- CTA is one action, in the last three seconds only, with where and by when.
