# Iteration metrics — reference for ad-performance-analyst

## Metrics (same set for every version)
Hook rate (3-second views / impressions), hold rate (watch-through), CTR,
CPC / CPM, saves, shares, comments, conversions. Do not compare a Reel to a
TVC on the same metric without noting the format.

## The drop-off second
Find the second people leave. Leave at second 2: the hook failed. Leave at
second 20: the payoff failed. That second is the problem — name it, do not guess.

## Comments are signal
One specific complaint repeated 10 times is a fix, not a feeling:
"too long", "can't hear", "where is the price?"

## Version 2 rules
Change one variable at a time, so you can learn from it. Keep what worked
(one hook, one frame, one line, one sound). Kill a losing version — do not
keep it alive out of politeness. A 15s reel is typically under 30 MB; a
version change on a code-rendered template takes minutes, not a re-edit.
