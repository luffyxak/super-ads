# Positioning — reference for ad-strategy

Read this only when positioning an ad. Keep it to one page.

## The five blanks
1. Audience in one line: who, where (city tier, language), what they already believe.
2. Problem in one line: what hurts, and why now.
3. One punch in five words max: the single idea a viewer repeats after one watch.
4. Proof: one fact, demo, price, or story, with a source and a date. No proof, no claim.
5. CTA in the last three seconds: what, where, by when.

## Enemy
Name what the ad is against: boring ads, high prices, slow apps, confusing plans.
Challenger energy needs an enemy. If there is no enemy, the ad has no edge.

## What we will NOT say
Three lines, written down. These stop the ad drifting into a second message.

## Test
Can a 12-year-old in Tier-2 India repeat the punch? If not, cut words until they can.
